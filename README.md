### Selected work

- **[Pipe handling VR simulator for Tenaris](https://audomaroduran.com/en/projects/simulador-hlo-tenaris)** · 2026<br>
  Standalone Meta Quest simulator that trains operators of high-capacity Hyster forklifts in a replica of a Tenaris
  yard. Co-architected and delivered in 12 weeks.
- **[Delivery truck simulator for Grupo Modelo](https://audomaroduran.com/en/projects/camion-reparto)** · 2026<br>
  The same build on PC and in VR, installed at two distribution centers one month after work began.
- **[Aracnet](https://audomaroduran.com/en/projects/aracnet)** · 2026<br>
  Co-op post-apocalyptic shooter in UE5. Technical architect of a 4-person team with an AI-assisted development
  pipeline.
- **[AWS Pixel Streaming for ARVISON](https://audomaroduran.com/en/projects/pixel-streaming)** · 2024<br>
  Unreal Engine instances launched on demand on AWS for interactive real-estate walkthroughs. Designed the
  architecture and led the 3-person team.
- **[DigiCampus](https://audomaroduran.com/en/projects/digicampus)** · 2024<br>
  Virtual campus streamed from AWS, with 200 seats at launch and instances that scale on demand.
- **[Penalty World](https://audomaroduran.com/en/projects/penalty-world)** · 2024<br>
  Vertical slice for Kronus Games: replicated turn-based multiplayer, GAS-based cards, and C++ exposed to Blueprint.
- **[RTG crane simulator for ATP](https://audomaroduran.com/en/projects/grua-rtg-atp)** · 2017<br>
  The first active RTG crane simulator in southern Tamaulipas, with a cabin, OEM controls, and an instructor station.
  It led to the founding of VIRTEC.
